## Design rules

- Abbreviations are always exactly three uppercase letters, unique across both pools.
- Each conference now has a 20-team design pool (40 total). A new save randomly draws 10 of each conference's 20 into that playthrough's actual league, so which teams exist varies game to game — see `drawLeagueTeams` in `lib/features/league/domain/league_draw.dart`. When a new franchise is created, the player chooses Atlantic or Pacific and replaces one randomly selected team from that conference's drawn 10.
- Each team needs a distinct primary color at a glance; exact palettes may change after logo and uniform work begins.
- Cities are real, but team names, brands, players, colleges, and league history are fictional.
- Keep team names clear to pronounce, distinctive in text, and separate from real professional basketball team names.
