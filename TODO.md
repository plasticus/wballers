## League & AI Teams

3. **AI teams' real coach quality isn't actually used for training.** Corrected 2026-08-12 -- this item originally (and wrongly) claimed AI teams have no generated coach at all. They do: `AiTeamRoster.coach` is real, generated at league creation via `generateCoach` (`league_generator.dart`), and `coachesByAbbreviation()` already exposes it for real in-game coaching bonuses. The actual, narrower gap: `resolveAiTeamSeasonTraining`/`resolveAiTeamSeasonEndAging` (`training_advancer.dart`) hardcode a flat `CoachStats.neutral` (development 50) instead of reading `aiTeam.coach.stats.development` -- every AI team's real, already-varied coach quality is used correctly for match-day bonuses but ignored for training/aging. Fix is small: read the real coach's development rating in both functions instead of the neutral fallback. Still relevant to the growth-curve levers shipped this session -- every AI-drafted prospect currently trains under the same effective coach quality regardless of who's actually coaching her, capping how many AI-team prospects can realistically reach the 90s tier.
