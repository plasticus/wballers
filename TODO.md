## League & AI Teams

2. **AI teams' real coach quality isn't actually used for training.** Corrected 2026-08-12 -- this item originally (and wrongly) claimed AI teams have no generated coach at all. They do: `AiTeamRoster.coach` is real, generated at league creation via `generateCoach` (`league_generator.dart`), and `coachesByAbbreviation()` already exposes it for real in-game coaching bonuses. The actual, narrower gap: `resolveAiTeamSeasonTraining`/`resolveAiTeamSeasonEndAging` (`training_advancer.dart`) hardcode a flat `CoachStats.neutral` (development 50) instead of reading `aiTeam.coach.stats.development` -- every AI team's real, already-varied coach quality is used correctly for match-day bonuses but ignored for training/aging. Fix is small: read the real coach's development rating in both functions instead of the neutral fallback. Still relevant to the growth-curve levers shipped this session -- every AI-drafted prospect currently trains under the same effective coach quality regardless of who's actually coaching her, capping how many AI-team prospects can realistically reach the 90s tier. (Coach of the Year/Executive of the Year, blocked on "AI teams have no coach" in the Awards catalog brainstorm, `0A_Completed.md` -- also worth re-checking against this correction; they may already be unblocked.)

## In-Game Strategy

6. **Offensive game plans for non-standard lineups.** A direct GM ask (2026-08-14), not ready to code: a legal lineup like PG/SG/SG/PF/C should be able to run a play style suited to it, GM-selectable. Current state: `match_engine.dart` never reads `Player.primaryPosition` at all, so a non-standard lineup already plays mechanically identically to a traditional one -- there's no positional/lineup-shape awareness in the sim yet for a game plan to even hook into. The roster-construction side now guarantees a real, position-covering starting five exists day 0 (`starting_roster_generator.dart`'s 4 narrative slots + the Day-0 free agent, shipped 2026-08-14) -- what's still missing is the match engine itself ever reading lineup shape at all. Needs (a) real lineup-shape awareness in the sim, then (b) a small GM-selectable set of offensive styles that shift how a given lineup's ratings get used (e.g. a small-ball plan leaning on `perimeterOffense`/pace for a center-light lineup, a post-up plan leaning on `interiorOffense` for a big one).
7. **Defensive strategy options.** A direct GM ask (2026-08-14), not ready to code -- GM wants a short, legible menu, not deep customization, and isn't a basketball expert so wants real options laid out. Brainstormed starting list (all map to rating-field tradeoffs the sim already has -- `interiorDefense`/`perimeterDefense`/`disruption`/`blocking`): focus coverage on the opponent's best player (double-team/face-guard -- trades team defense elsewhere to shut one player down), pack the paint (trades perimeter defense for interior defense against a big front-court team), extend/pressure the perimeter (opposite tradeoff, against a shooting-heavy team), and a neutral balanced default. Same dependency as item 6.
8. **Where should game-plan/defense screens live?** A direct GM ask (2026-08-14), open design question. A pre-game popup doesn't give the GM room to actually study the opponent first; a Team-screen-only setting risks being set once and forgotten, never adjusted per-opponent. Leading idea, not yet decided: a sticky default set from the Team/Roster screen (survives game to game, so it's never blank) with an optional per-game override surfaced directly on the "Matchup Analysis" pre-game screen (`match_preview_screen.dart`, shipped 2026-08-12) -- that screen already shows the exact opponent breakdown (Team Strength, Top 3) a GM would want in view while making the call. Needs a real design pass.
