   - **Blocker resolved and built (2026-08-17): stamina/fatigue system implemented, tuned, wired into live gameplay, and validated.** Real code now (`lib/features/match/engine/fatigue.dart`, wired into `match_engine.dart`, `MatchResult.finalEnergy`) -- full writeup in `0B_Planned.md`'s stamina appendix, this is the short version. Formulas were hand-checked against `substitution_policy.dart`'s target-minutes table first, then validated with a real diagnostic (`tool/fatigue_diagnostic.dart`, run via `flutter test tool/fatigue_diagnostic.dart`) across 20 real games with real generated rosters -- twice: once to retune the drain/recovery rates (the initial rates left even the heaviest-used rotation tier under a 2% average penalty, too mild for a direct GM ask that ordinary heavy-minute play should feel it), and again after wiring the resulting penalty into every `possession_engine.dart` rating contest a fatigued player touches (passing, shooting, free throws, blocking, rebounding, both ends of the floor -- `_fatigueBonus`, the same accumulator slot `OffenseShapeBonus`/`DefenseTacticBonus`/`coachMatchupBonus` share) to confirm the existing scoring calibration (~201 combined avg, `match_engine.dart`'s own doc comment) survived: it did, 205.1 avg across the 20-game post-wiring run, comfortably within normal noise. Final numbers: top two rotation tiers (30/26-minute targets) take a 6-9% average penalty on nearly all their player-games, with a small tail into severe (<50 energy) territory; everything at 14 minutes and under stays essentially untouched. `MatchResult.finalEnergy` is kept on the result on purpose, past what gameplay itself needs, for a later post-game consumer -- e.g. a "most tired players" callout to help the GM decide who to move down the rotation (a direct GM ask, 2026-08-17); no such consumer exists yet, this just keeps the door open without inventing save-schema persistence nobody's asked for (same transient, game-scoped lifetime as the box score). **Still open**: substitutions still don't react to fatigue -- `pickOnCourt`/`targetMinutesFor` are driven purely by target minutes and foul-outs, no energy-aware bench logic yet. That, plus a real UI consumer of `finalEnergy`, are natural follow-ups once there's appetite -- and the quarter-break coaching-options catalog (this item's other half, above) now has real stamina-related levers to draw on instead of being blocked on this system existing.
