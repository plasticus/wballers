## Draft-pool guidelines

- **Premier:** regularly produces high-lottery and first-round prospects.
- **Strong:** regularly produces draftable players, with occasional stars.
- **Developing:** produces fewer elite prospects but remains an important source of late picks and unexpected breakouts.
- School prestige influences prospect exposure and starting readiness; potential, growth, and player outcomes must still be generated independently.
- Codes are unique, three-letter identifiers for design and display. Retain that uniqueness when the list changes.
